Privacy

Updated September 21, 2026

This page explains what data of yours I keep, what for, and how you get it out. It's written to be understood, not to confuse you.

Who I am

I'm Nidia, and I run Lumina IA Studio, part of Wave Plus Pro, in the Washington DC area. For anything on this page, write to wavemarketingone@gmail.com.

What I ask you for

Two things only: your name and your email address, and only if you type them into the form. Nothing else. No phone, no address, no card details.

If you join the WhatsApp community, your number and profile name are visible there — that's handled by WhatsApp, not by me, and it works like any other group.

What I use them for

  • Sending you access to the community.
  • Writing to you when there's something worth writing about: a tool that changed, something I figured out, or when Lumina opens.

That's it. I don't sell your email, lend it, or trade it with anyone.

Where it's stored

The list lives in Mailchimp, a US company that does this for a living. They store the data on their servers and have their own privacy policy. I go into that list to write to you, nothing more.

How you leave

At the bottom of any email from me there's an unsubscribe link. One click and you're out, no questions and nobody writing to ask you why.

If you also want your data deleted entirely, write to me and I'll delete it. I won't ask for an explanation.

Cookies and tracking

This page uses no advertising cookies and does not follow you around the internet. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, which records the request like any site that loads a font.

The links I share on each account are different, so I know which of my accounts you came from. That's the only thing I measure, and it's to know where it's worth posting.

Age

This is for people 18 and over. If you're younger, don't leave your details here.

If anything changes

If I change any of this, I update the date above. If the change matters, I'll email you first.
